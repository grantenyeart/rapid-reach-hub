Part 1 · where the no turns into a yes
  1. 15:25

    The flat no, and the question that beat it. "I don't want AI. I do not want AI. I refuse to do that. That's garbage." Grant does not argue with him. He asks "have you ever experimented with AI at all?", digs out the bad experience underneath the belief, then reframes the product as a gatekeeper that only passes real calls through. Michael: "Yeah, I kind of like that." That entire turn takes about a minute.

  2. 39:55

    He stops talking and dials it. Puts the AI on speaker in the middle of the call and lets it work a price objection and a vomit cleanup. Michael: "I didn't realize that it could overcome objections and actually pin down a price and pin down a day." Everything before this was conversation. This is the minute that moved him.

  3. 45:55

    Price, and only now. "The receptionist is normally a thousand. If you're getting the meta ads and the Google ads, I'll do 50% off to implement all three." The discount bought a bigger deal instead of shrinking one. Watch the order: he never put a number on it until after the demo had already landed.

  4. 52:08

    He pays for the thing he called garbage. Thirty seven minutes from "I refuse" to money on the table, and nobody out-argued him to get there. He heard it work.

Part 2 · the paperwork call, same day
  1. 20:00

    The demo breaks, live, in front of the client. The AI had been flipped into gatekeeper mode on their end and refused to quote a price. Grant names exactly what happened, ties it back to the gatekeeper idea Michael already said he liked, and keeps going. No panic, no apology spiral. Yours will break one day too.

  2. 24:00

    The commitment, in order, AI last. "Get those signed and sent back, I'm going to send you the payment plan for the meta ads. And then I'll send you the upfront payment for the AI." Everybody knows what happens, in what sequence, and who does it.

Watch the moves, not the money. This call is from June. The receptionist ladder is now $1,500, $1,000 and $500, and we do not sell Meta ads at all any more, so put the Google Search Campaign in wherever you hear Meta. How he handles the objection is what you are here for, not what he charged for it.

The one thing to remember. Lead with freedom, not technology. "You never have to take a phone call again" sells. "Conversational AI voice agent" does not. The full version is the upsell, the missed-calls option is the easy yes, and either way the goal of the call is the Zoom.
