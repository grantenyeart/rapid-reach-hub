The Voice AI cold call script

Sell the freedom, not the tech. The goal of this call is one thing: book the Zoom.

1The pivot in

"We're booked" or "it's just me" is not a no. It is your cue to switch to this pitch. A booked solo detailer cannot answer the phone with his hands in a car, so he is losing jobs he already gets calls for. Do not sell more leads. Sell capture.

Say this

"Honestly, that's exactly why I call the good ones. Real quick, when you're out there mid-detail and the phone rings, are you catching those or do they go to voicemail?

Yeah, that's where the money leaks for solo guys. You don't need more leads, you're already getting calls you can't physically answer. We set up something that answers every one in your voice and books it right into your calendar, so you stop handing those jobs to the next guy."

If they mention a slow season

"And a lot of the guys we work with use the slow months to pull past customers back in automatically, so it's not so slow."

2The opener

Same on every call, every product.

Say this

"Hey, is this [Company]?"

let them say yes

"Perfect. I was wondering, do you guys do ceramic coatings, or just detailing?"

let them answer. this one question decides the rest of the call

"Okay, perfect. Well, I was actually calling because I'm looking to partner up with someone in the area to bring in more work. And honestly, it's really hard to find good detailers in your area, and you guys stood out to me."

now ask for the number, in their words

They do ceramics: "How many more ceramic coatings could you take on per week?"
Detailing only: "How many more details could you take on per week?"

3Why this opener works

The ceramic question is not small talk. It is the cheapest qualifying question on the call. Their answer tells you which product to sell, which price to quote, and which word to use for the rest of the conversation. Ask it before anything else.

"Partner up," not "sell you." You are not pitching, you are looking for someone to work with. Nobody hangs up on a partnership.

"Hard to find good detailers, and you guys stood out." They were chosen, not dialed. That one line is the difference between a cold call and a compliment, and it buys you the next ninety seconds.

Ask in their word. If they said ceramics, ask about ceramics. If they said detailing, ask about details. Using their word proves you listened and makes the number easy to give.

4No number, no diagnosis

The rule

You need a number before you diagnose anything.

They give you a number: they have room, keep going.
Booked out for weeks: they have money and a capacity problem, and this is your AI receptionist call.

No number, no diagnosis.

5The first diagnostic

Once you have the number, this is the question that opens up this product. It used to be the opener. It is a better diagnostic.

Say this

"Quick question, what time do you guys close?"

let them answer

"Okay, got it. And if I gave you guys a call past [that time], would that go straight to voicemail, or do you have something in place that answers that for you?"

Reading the answer

Straight to voicemail is your call. Every call that hits voicemail after they close is usually a booking that walked to the next guy.

They have something: "oh nice, what are you using for that?" If it does not book the job into the calendar, run the same pitch.

6The pitch

After they confirm they handle calls themselves, or that they miss them.

Say this

"If I told you I could automate that fully out, so every call gets booked directly into your calendar and you never have to take a phone call again, would that be something you'd be interested in?"

If they hesitate, give two options

"We can either set it up full, so the AI answers every call and books them in, or we can set it up so it only catches your missed calls. You'd never have a missed call again either way."

Why two options

Lead with freedom, "never take a phone call again," not tech. The full version is the upsell, the missed-calls option is the easy yes. Let them pick, then go straight to the Zoom.

7Objections

Handle it, then steer to the Zoom. Every time.

"How do I know this works?"
"We're already doing this for detailers across the U.S. Higher rankings, consistent bookings, repeat customers."
"How much is it?"
"Our pricing and packages vary based on what you're looking for, and that's exactly what the call is for. They'll show you what makes sense for your shop."
"I need to think about it."
"Totally. Is it the money, the timing, or just wanting to see how it works first?"
"Just send me info."
"Easiest way is a quick Zoom with one of our specialists, they'll walk it visually. Mornings or afternoons?"

8When they will not let the price go

The rule

You do not quote a price on a setting call. Pricing lives on the Zoom. Your job is to get them there, not to win the price conversation on the phone.

  1. First ask. "Pricing depends which direction we go, so honestly that's exactly what the call is for. We'll build a plan around your business and break the numbers down there."
  2. They push again. "It really depends on your competition and what you're trying to hit. There's a range, and there are options with no money up front. Let's hop on the call, break it down, and find a plan that makes sense for both of us."
  3. Hail Mary, only when they are about to hang up. "Alright, look. We can get you set up with no money up front. Worst case you see what we do and it costs you nothing."

9Book the Zoom

Say this

"I'll get a quick Zoom set up with one of our specialists who'll show you exactly what it looks like and go over everything in a bit more depth. Do you have any availability today?"

  1. Push same day first. If no, go to tomorrow.
  2. Pin a specific time. "Morning or evening?" then "What about 1pm / 8pm?"
  3. Get their email, send the Zoom link now, give your number. "Text me if anything comes up."

10Ending

Say this

"Perfect, you're all set. One of our specialists will walk through everything and show you exactly how we set this up for detailing companies. At the end of the call they'll also send you a free full Google optimization video. Great talking to you, speak soon!"

Then

Put the booking in the bookings tab in Discord straight after the call.
