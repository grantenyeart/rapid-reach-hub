================================================================
KILLING NO-SHOWS - VIDEO SCRIPT
Run with the deck: grantenyeart.github.io/no-shows
Target length: 7 to 9 minutes
================================================================

HOW TO USE THIS
Do not read it. The bold lines are the ones worth saying close to
word for word. Everything else is the point you are making, say it
your way. Start the recording with the deck already on screen.


================================================================
SLIDE 1 - KILLING NO-SHOWS                            about 0:30
================================================================

What is going on everyone. Today we are going over how we kill
no-shows and get our show rate up, because right now that is the
one thing standing between the work we are already doing and the
money we should be making from it.

Here is the frame for the whole video.

   "THE BOOKED CALL IS NOT THE WIN. THE CALL THEY ACTUALLY TURN
    UP TO IS THE WIN."

A booked call that no-shows is worth exactly nothing. It is not
half a win. It is zero, and it cost you everything it took to get
it. So everything in this video is about the gap between booking
it and them actually being there.


================================================================
SLIDE 2 - NOBODY FORGETS A CALL THEY WANT TO BE ON    about 1:00
================================================================

Start here, because if you get this wrong nothing else works.

   "NOBODY FORGETS A CALL THEY ACTUALLY WANT TO BE ON."

A no-show is almost never a calendar problem. They did not lose
the invite. They were not confused about the time. Somewhere
between your call and that morning they decided that half an hour
with us was not worth it.

That is the whole thing. It is not a reminder problem, it is a
value problem.

   -> SLOW DOWN HERE, this is the point the video turns on

Which means the show does not get won in the reminder. It gets
won on the setting call. By the time you are texting them that
morning, the decision is already made. All your reminders do is
collect it.

So the six things we are about to go through are either building
that decision or protecting it. Three before you hang up, three
after.


================================================================
SLIDE 3 - SIX THINGS, EVERY SINGLE TIME               about 0:45
================================================================

Here is the whole process. Six things, every single call, no
exceptions.

Walk the list, one line each, do not explain yet:

   One. Earn the show before you book it.
   Two. Book a consultation, not a sales call.
   Three. Ask the zombie apocalypse question.
   Four. Get the reply YES before you hang up.
   Five. Run the confirmation sequence.
   Six. Be unreasonable about the follow up.

One through four happen before you hang up the phone. Five and
six happen after. Let us go through them.


================================================================
SLIDE 4 - STEP 1, EARN THE SHOW                       about 1:15
================================================================

Three things have to be true before a call is worth putting on a
closer's calendar.

   "IF YOU CANNOT ANSWER ALL THREE, YOU HAVE NOT BOOKED A CALL.
    YOU HAVE BOOKED A NO-SHOW."

One. Did they tell you the pain, in their own words. Not "yeah I
could use more work." That is nothing. I mean the actual problem.
Slow weeks. An empty calendar. An agency that burned them. A van
sitting in the driveway.

   Here is the test: can you repeat their pain back to the
   closer? If you cannot, they never gave you any.

Two. Do they actually need what we sell. Somebody booked out six
weeks and happy is not a buyer. Somebody who wants more work and
is not getting it, that is a buyer.

Three. Are you talking to the decision maker. If they have to run
it past a partner or an owner, that call is not closing, and most
of the time it is not even happening.

   -> THE WHY, say this in your own words

A prospect who just described his own problem out loud has a
reason to show up. Somebody who agreed to a call to get you off
the phone does not.


================================================================
SLIDE 5 - STEP 2, CONSULTATION NOT A SALES CALL       about 1:15
================================================================

This one changes your show rate more than anything else on this
list.

   "NOBODY BLOCKS OUT HALF AN HOUR TO BE SOLD TO. THEY WILL BLOCK
    OUT HALF AN HOUR TO FIND OUT WHAT IS WRONG WITH THEIR
    BUSINESS."

You are not booking a closing call. You are booking a free
consultation and a free audit. On that call we go through:

   Their Google Business Profile, what is set up wrong on it and
   what that is costing them.

   Their SEO and where they actually rank, who is sitting above
   them in their own town and why.

   How much work is slipping past them right now, the searches
   happening in their area going to somebody else.

And say this part out loud on the phone, do not assume they know:

   "THEY KEEP THAT AUDIT WHETHER THEY BUY OR NOT."

Worst case they get a free teardown of their business and learn
what to fix. Best case we fix it for them. Either way they are
not showing up to get pitched, they are showing up to get
something.


================================================================
SLIDE 6 - STEP 3, THE ZOMBIE APOCALYPSE QUESTION      about 1:00
================================================================

Now before you hang up, you make them commit out loud.

   "OTHER THAN A ZOMBIE APOCALYPSE, IS THERE ANY OTHER REASON YOU
    WOULD NOT BE ABLE TO MAKE THIS CALL?"

   -> SAY IT THE WAY YOU WOULD ON A CALL, light, not serious

It works because it is light enough that they laugh, and specific
enough that they have to actually answer.

You get one of two things, and both of them are wins.

Either you get a clean "no, I will be there," and that is now a
promise they would have to break to your face.

Or they tell you the real conflict. "Actually I have a detail at
six." Perfect, let us move it. Now you have a call at a time they
can genuinely make.

   "BOTH OF THOSE BEAT FINDING OUT AT 2PM TOMORROW."


================================================================
SLIDE 7 - STEP 4, GET THE REPLY YES                   about 1:00
================================================================

The second you book them, they get a text. Two testimonial
videos, where the call is, and a line asking them to reply YES to
confirm.

Most people send that text and hang up and hope. Do not do that.
Walk them to it while you still have them on the phone.

   "I JUST SENT YOU A TEXT. CAN YOU OPEN IT AND REPLY YES SO I
    KNOW THE SYSTEM HAS YOU LOCKED IN?"

Then wait for it. Do not fill the silence, just wait.

   -> THE WHY

A confirmation you watched them send beats ten reminders you sent
into silence. And if they will not open a text while they are on
the phone with you, that is telling you right now that this call
is in trouble, and you would rather know now than tomorrow.

Micro agreements turn into big closes. This is the first one.


================================================================
SLIDE 8 - STEP 5, THE CONFIRMATION SEQUENCE           about 1:00
================================================================

Now the part after you hang up. Run every one of these. Not the
ones that feel comfortable. All of them.

   On the call. Text sent, replied YES, watched, before you hang
   up.

   That morning. Text them. Short, warm, no pressure. "Looking
   forward to this afternoon, we have done some research and got
   some ideas for you."

   That morning. Call them. Not instead of the text. As well as
   the text.

   Fifteen minutes before. Always. "Hey, looking forward to our
   call, just want to confirm you are still good to jump on."

   No answer. Always leave a voicemail.

   -> HIT THE VOICEMAIL POINT HARD

Never skip the voicemail. If you do not leave one you look
unconfident in what you are calling about. I am confident in my
product, so I am going to follow up, and I am going to leave a
message. A missed ring is nothing. A voice they recognise is a
reason to pick up next time.


================================================================
SLIDE 9 - STEP 6, BE UNREASONABLE                     about 1:15
================================================================

Last one, and this is a standard, not a technique.

You dialed for days to get this person on the phone. You got them
talking about the thing that keeps them up at night. You got it
on a closer's calendar. And then you let it die because you did
not want to seem pushy on the fourth attempt.

   "FIFTEEN CALLS IN THREE DAYS IF THAT IS WHAT IT TAKES."

   -> THE LINE, say it slow

If somebody had a gun to your head, you would not be reasonable
about it. You would not look at the gun and try to reason about
why they are holding it. You would do everything you could.

That is the energy. This is the most valuable half hour anybody
on this team produces all week, so chase it like it is.

And understand why you are allowed to be that aggressive. You
believe in what you are calling about. We have taken companies to
their first ten thousand dollar month. We have doubled businesses
that were already running. If you actually believe that, why
would you let somebody walk away from a free consultation that
could change their company?

   "YOU ARE NOT CHASING THEM FOR YOU. YOU ARE CHASING THEM FOR
    THE THING YOU KNOW YOU CAN DO FOR THEM."


================================================================
SLIDE 10 - THE CHECKLIST                              about 0:45
================================================================

So before any call counts as booked, this is the list. Read it
off, do not add to it:

   They told me their pain in their own words and I can repeat it
   back.
   They actually need this, they are not booked out and happy.
   I am talking to the decision maker.
   I booked a free consultation and audit, not a sales call, and
   I said so.
   I asked the zombie apocalypse question and got a clean yes.
   I watched them reply YES before I hung up.
   Morning text, morning call, fifteen minutes before, voicemail
   every time.
   I will chase it until they show or until they tell me no.

If you cannot tick all eight, it is not a booked call yet.


================================================================
SLIDE 11 - THE CLOSE                                  about 0:30
================================================================

   "BOOKING THE CALL IS THE EASY HALF."

Anyone on this team can get a yes on the phone. That is not the
hard part and it is not what you are paid for.

The job is getting them to still want it tomorrow afternoon. And
that gets decided by what you did before you hung up, not by how
many times you text them the next day.

   "OWN THE SHOW."

Go get them on the call.
