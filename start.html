Rapid Reach · Day one

What this job actually is.

Read this before you touch a phone or sit on a Zoom. Ten minutes, and the rest of the training will make a lot more sense.

1Who we sell to

Local service businesses. Mobile detailing is most of the book, and we also sell window cleaning, commercial cleaning, spray foam and epoxy. Owner operators and small crews, mostly one person who answers their own phone.

That matters more than it sounds. You are not calling a marketing department. You are calling somebody who is probably under a truck right now, who has been burned by an agency before, and who makes every decision themselves. No committee, no procurement. If they believe you, they can say yes on the call.

2What we sell them

We get them more jobs. The main product is a Google Search Campaign, which puts them in front of people actively searching for what they do, on a page built to turn that click into a booking. Around that we sell websites, AI receptionists, a growth system and more. Every one of them is in the Products section of the hub.

Say Google Search Campaign, never PPC. And never say LSA, say "your Google pay per lead ads." Clients do not speak in acronyms and the ones who do are usually testing you.

3The two roles

Setter

Get the meeting

Cold calls all day. Your job is not to sell the product. It is to find somebody with a problem we fix, make them feel it, and book them onto a Zoom with a closer. You win when they show up.

Closer

Get the deal

You run the Zoom. Diagnose, show them what we build, handle what comes up and ask for the business. You win when they sign and stay.

Most people start as a setter. It is the faster way to learn, because you will have more sales conversations in a week than most people have in a month.

4What sales actually is here

It is not convincing. Nobody on this phone is going to be talked into spending money they did not want to spend.

The job is diagnosis. Find out where their jobs come from now, what that is costing them, what happens if nothing changes. If what we sell genuinely fixes it, the sale is mostly a matter of making that obvious. If it does not fix it, you let them go and call the next one. The second part is what separates people who last from people who burn out.

The why tells you how to close. Do not just collect what they want. Find out why they want it. Somebody who wants more jobs because they are trying to quit their day job buys differently than somebody who just wants a busier Tuesday.

Read that again. Most of your day is dials that go nowhere, and that is the job working correctly, not the job failing. The close rate on a live call is a coin flip in your favour. Everything in this hub exists to get you more live calls and make that coin heavier.

6How you get paid

Commission, per service, and it is not a mystery. The Services Playbook lists every product with the real dollar payout next to it. Read it early. Knowing exactly what a deal puts in your pocket changes how you talk about it.

7What good looks like in week one

  • You hit your dials. Activity is the only thing fully in your control. Do that part and the rest follows.
  • You sound like a person. Scripts are a floor, not a ceiling. Learn them so well you stop thinking about them.
  • You book one. One booked call in week one is a real week.
  • You listen back. Pull one of your own calls and listen to it. Nobody improves on this phone without hearing themselves.
  • You ask. Nobody expects you to know this yet. Ask before you guess on a live call.
