The Core Frame

"Looks good" vs. "performs good"

You are not selling a website. You are selling a revenue-generating machine that books jobs month after month.

Looks good

Pretty pictures. That is it. It sits there and books nothing. Pure vanity.

Performs good

Converts visitors into booked jobs, AND gets found on Google and on AI. That is money.
Know Your Prospect

Two types of prospects

No website yet
The easy land. Sell a clean, good-looking site first. Upsell the AI visibility later. Land and expand.
Already has a website
The teardown play. They care that it looks good. Your job is to show them it does not perform.

Talk to their level. If they could not build a simple site, "AEO" and "LLMs.txt" sail right over their head. Say it plain: "It needs to rank on Google AND show up when someone asks ChatGPT."

The Teardown Play

How to run a website they already have

1
Share their site on your screen
2
Run the checklist, call out the red flags
3
The phone test: switch to mobile view
4
Show one of ours (the demo)
5
Drop the line, then go quiet

The phone test wins deals. 85% of their customers are on a phone, and that is exactly where their site looks broken.

What "Performs" Means

It converts AND it gets found

Converts
High-converting layout, booking buttons, contact forms, instant lead response, mobile-first, fast load.
Gets found
SEO gets you found on Google. AEO gets you found on AI: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity.

By default, every website is invisible to AI. We build yours to do both.

Your Proof On Screen

The 3 AI-visibility checks

1
/llms.txt
Theirs: blank or 100 words. Ours: a full file with Q&A, key pages, and every city. That is what ChatGPT reads.
2
/robots.txt
Which AI crawlers are allowed in. Theirs: usually WordPress only. Ours: Claude, GPT, Perplexity, Gemini, all enabled.
3
/sitemap.xml
The map of the whole site. Most are missing or unoptimized. Ours is built right.

Pricing & How To Sell It

Two ways to sell a website

Standalone
Basic (looks good): $500 + $50/mo. Optimized: $1,000 to $1,500 + $50/mo. Anchor it: "our sites are $2,000." Monthly financing available.

Bundle (the goal)

Include the website "free" with DGS ($500 to $1,000/mo). Bump the price a little, frame it as $2,000 of value for free. Every client should end up here.

Objection Playbook

The 6 you'll hear

"Perfect, let's look at it together right now." Then tear it down.

"Compared to what? It costs more NOT to have one. You lose every lead that doesn't convert."

Pro vs. Amateur

What closes vs. what stalls

Do

  • Tear down their current site live
  • Run the phone test every time
  • Get the average ticket first
  • Show real proof (Matrix)
  • Anchor high, then include it
  • Always have a demo ready

Don't

  • Dump features and jargon
  • Sell "a website" instead of jobs
  • Quote price before the ticket
  • Argue that you're worth it
  • Talk above their head
  • Leave without showing a demo
The One-Line Lesson

Don't sell a website.
Sell more booked jobs.

Tear down what they have. Show them what performs. Do the math on one extra job. Then go quiet and let them say yes.

Now go sell.
